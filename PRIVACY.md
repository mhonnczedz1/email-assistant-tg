# Privacy Policy: Email Assistant TG

Last updated: November 2026

Email Assistant TG is a personal email assistant used by a small group of invited people.

## What it accesses
With your consent through Google sign-in, it reads your Gmail messages and metadata (sender, subject, text, labels, attachments' text where needed), and it can apply labels, archive mail, and create reply drafts. It requests one Google permission, `gmail.modify`. It sends an email only after you explicitly approve that specific message, and it never deletes mail.

## How it uses your data
Only to provide the service to you: sorting and labeling mail, alerting you about emails that need a decision, creating reminders, and drafting replies. Message text is processed by third-party AI models through a provider configured not to log or train on it. Your data is never sold, rented, or shared for advertising.

## Where it is stored
On the operator's own computer, in a separate area for each person. Google sign-in tokens are stored there too. The operator can technically access this data.

## How long, and how to delete it
Data is kept while you use the service. You can revoke access at any time in your Google account (Security, then third-party access), and you can ask the operator at mhonn.ybanez@gmail.com to delete all your data. It will be removed on request.

## Google API Services User Data Policy
Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
