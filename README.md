# Email Assistant TG

A personal email assistant for a small group of invited people. It connects to your Gmail account (with your consent through Google sign-in) and, through a Telegram chat, sorts and labels your mail, alerts you about emails that need a decision, reminds you about bills and flights, and drafts replies for your approval. It never sends an email without your explicit approval.

Access is invite-only and set up by hand by the operator. To ask for access or to remove your data, contact mhonn.ybanez@gmail.com.

See [PRIVACY.md](PRIVACY.md) for how your data is handled.
